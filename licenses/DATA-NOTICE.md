The source records are from ASE 3.29.0's merged G2/97 database (ase.data.g2,
g2_1.py and g2_2.py), with experimental formation enthalpies and calculated
ZPE/thermal corrections, and MP2(full)/6-31G(d) geometries. The original ASE
LGPL-2.1-or-later notice is retained in ASE-NOTICE.txt. For redistribution of
the extracted/derived data in this repository we elect its LGPL-3.0 option.
The original package sources, their exact hashes and every included record
are identified by data/split.json. Original references include
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.477422 and J. Chem. Phys. 119, 12129 (2003).
Original new model/training code and correction coefficients are BSD-3-Clause.
Upstream ORB code/checkpoint remain Apache-2.0 and are downloaded separately.
